Privacy Policy

Survey Contour Generator (ContourGen) · Developer: MakeshTech · Last updated: 22 November 2026

Short version: the app does not collect, store or share any personal data. Your survey data stays on your device.

Survey Contour Generator (“the app”) is a tool that turns survey point data into contour maps. This policy explains what the app does with your data.

Data the app handles

  • Survey files you choose. When you import an Excel (.xlsx) file, the app reads it on your device using the system file picker, only the file you select. The data is processed in memory on your device to draw the map and statistics.
  • Files you export. DXF, AutoLISP, script, image, Excel and print outputs are created on your device and saved only to the location you choose. They are never uploaded.

Data the app does not collect

The app does not collect or transmit personal information, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, advertising IDs, usage analytics or crash reports. It does not require an account.

Network access

The Android app does not request internet permission and cannot send data off your device. The web version, if you use it, loads its own files from the hosting site and then runs entirely in your browser; your survey data is not sent to any server.

Third parties

The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking software and shares no data with third parties.

Storage and security

Survey data exists only in the app’s memory while it is open and in files you export. Closing the app discards the in-memory data. Android backup is disabled for the app.

Children

The app is a professional surveying tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with an updated date.
